# Publication Strategy Memory Policy

This policy owns the use and maintenance of reusable publication strategy experience. The [library](./publication_route_memory_library.md) owns the card bodies; `contracts/memory_descriptor.json` owns discovery and locator metadata.

## Use

Read a small set of relevant cards as context for current Stage reasoning. Cards describe fit, poor fit, evidence, analysis/display patterns, claim limits, reviewer risks and failed paths. They do not score routes, choose a winning hypothesis, replace evidence, or authorize quality, publication, submission or artifact mutation.

Card identities and source locators support retrieval. Their headings and prose are not a parser API or a quality gate; usefulness is assessed by medical reasoning. Current callable surfaces remain in the action catalog and handler registry. There is no retained MAS seed-apply, inventory, overlay or memory-router implementation to invoke.

## Writeback

Propose a lesson only when it generalizes beyond the current paper. Preserve source/provenance, applicability, limitations, failed-path evidence and the reason for acceptance or rejection. Current-study results, reviewer findings and publication status remain in their own evidence/quality records.

MAS owns the Markdown body and accepts or rejects changes. Workspace cards and receipts belong to the workspace, not this repository. OPL can transport and index body-free locators, freshness and receipt refs; it cannot accept writeback, copy medical memory into platform authority or choose a research route.

## Migration Plan

A workspace refresh compares the current canonical library with its accepted local lessons and records which candidates are adopted, rejected or superseded under MAS authority. Preserve existing accepted content and provenance; do not overwrite a workspace pack merely because a newer seed index exists.

This is an owner-governed content operation, not a promise of a legacy command or automatic initializer. Resolve the actual workspace and permitted write scope before applying. The descriptor's migration-policy locator points here; package presence and docs do not prove workspace application.

## Maintenance

Retire superseded cards after transferring unique lessons and repairing their source/index references. Do not maintain a second archetype library, a migration completion list or a historical receipt ledger here. [Markdown-first policy](../domain_memory_markdown_first_policy.md) governs the shared body/index distinction.
