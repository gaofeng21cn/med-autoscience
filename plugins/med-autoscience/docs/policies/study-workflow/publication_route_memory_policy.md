# Publication Strategy Memory Policy

The [library](../../../agent/knowledge/publication_strategy_library.md) owns reusable research strategy bodies. OPL owns the shared catalog and scoped recall; [MAS knowledge policy](../domain_memory_markdown_first_policy.md) defines the domain's placement and acceptance rules.

Use relevant patterns to compare fit, evidence requirements, analysis and display choices, claim limits, reviewer risks and stop conditions. Clinical endpoints, cohorts and actual study results must come from current study evidence. A pattern cannot choose a winning hypothesis or authorize publication.

Store an individual paper's decisions as project-scoped knowledge in its canonical source. A reviewed lesson that applies across papers may enter the disease topic, while an accepted team policy belongs to owner scope; a reusable, non-private method can be proposed for the versioned product knowledge pack. These scopes do not prescribe a directory. An existing Obsidian library and ordinary project Markdown use the same source-binding mechanism, without an intermediate full-body mirror. Include supporting and failed-path evidence, scope, content kind and review dates. MAS and the appropriate knowledge owner retain semantic acceptance; OPL validates catalog structure, source/body identity, access and consumption receipts.

Maintain one canonical body per lesson and write accepted updates back to that body. The proposal names `source_refs`, `target_scope: {kind, id}`, `content_kind`, `proposed_rule_or_mechanism`, `evidence_class` and `review_requirement`. Supersede an obsolete entry and record its replacement; use the shared lifecycle instead of copying Agent seed packs into workspaces. Source edits, expiry and revoked access invalidate dependent current summaries and require a new accepted version before reuse. Literature originals and study evidence remain in their evidence stores.
